
## 1. Return Policy

Buyers may request a return within 7 days of receiving an eligible product.

A return may be accepted when the product is significantly different from the seller's description, arrives damaged, or the wrong product was delivered.

Products that are correctly described and match the listing may not be eligible for return.

Return requests must include a clear explanation of the issue. The marketplace may request photographs or other evidence before approving a return.

Some product categories may have different return conditions. Buyers should check the applicable category-specific policy before making a purchase.

---

## 2. Refund Policy

A refund may be issued when an approved return is completed or when a seller fails to deliver a purchased product.

If a seller does not deliver the product, the buyer may request a refund through the marketplace support process.

Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment method and verification process.

The marketplace does not guarantee an immediate refund after a request is submitted. Refunds are processed after the request has been reviewed and approved.

---

## 3. Seller Policy

Sellers must provide accurate information about the products they list.

Sellers must not provide false information about a product's condition, specifications, ownership, price, or availability.

Sellers should upload clear photographs that accurately represent the product.

Sellers must communicate honestly with buyers and should not request payment outside the approved marketplace payment process when marketplace payment is available.

A seller may be responsible for resolving issues caused by inaccurate or misleading information in a listing.

---

## 4. Buyer Policy

Buyers should carefully review the product title, description, price, condition, photographs, and other available information before purchasing.

Buyers should communicate with sellers through the marketplace when possible.

Buyers should not share sensitive personal information, passwords, PINs, or authentication codes with sellers.

Buyers should report suspicious activity, misleading listings, or suspected fraud to marketplace support.

Buyers should verify that a product meets their requirements before completing a purchase.

---

## 5. Prohibited Items Policy

Users must not list products that are illegal or prohibited by marketplace rules.

Weapons, illegal drugs, stolen goods, counterfeit products, and other illegal items are prohibited.

Products that infringe intellectual property rights may not be listed.

Listings containing fraudulent, misleading, or deceptive information may be removed.

The marketplace may remove prohibited listings and may restrict accounts that repeatedly violate marketplace policies.

---

## 6. Listing Policy

Every listing must contain accurate and relevant information about the product being offered.

The title and description must represent the actual product.

Sellers must not use misleading titles or descriptions to attract buyers.

The listed price should represent the actual expected selling price.

Duplicate listings for the same product may be removed.

The marketplace may modify or remove listings that violate its policies.

---

## 7. Payment Safety Policy

Users should use the marketplace's approved payment methods when available.

Users should not share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or banking authentication information with another user.

The marketplace will never require a user to disclose their password or authentication code to complete a transaction.

Users should report suspicious payment requests or links to marketplace support.

Transactions conducted outside the marketplace may not be covered by marketplace buyer protection.

---

## 8. Account and Fraud Policy

Users must provide truthful information when creating and using marketplace accounts.

Creating accounts for fraudulent activities is prohibited.

Users must not impersonate another person or organization.

Users must not attempt to manipulate prices, reviews, transactions, or marketplace systems.

Accounts involved in fraudulent or abusive activity may be restricted or suspended.

Users should report suspected fraud or unauthorized account activity to marketplace support.
